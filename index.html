1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Escape Protocol: Daily Puzzles (“Escape Protocol”, “the Game”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the Game.

The Game is published by Untouchable Studios.

2. Information we collect

Escape Protocol is designed to collect as little personal information as reasonably necessary.

We do not intentionally collect

  • your real name;
  • your email address;
  • your phone number;
  • precise or approximate location;
  • contacts;
  • photos or videos;
  • advertising ID;
  • device serial numbers;
  • payment card information;
  • social-media information.

Gameplay and telemetry data

To understand how the Game performs and to improve level balance, progression and usability, the Game may collect pseudonymous gameplay information such as:

  • a randomly generated or Supabase-assigned user identifier;
  • session start and end times and session duration;
  • levels started and completed;
  • level attempts and completion times;
  • number of moves and stars earned;
  • use of hints, undo and restart;
  • campaign progression;
  • Daily Protocol participation and completion;
  • cosmetic rewards unlocked or selected;
  • interaction with certain Game features;
  • Game version and build version;
  • limited technical information needed to diagnose errors and synchronize telemetry.

This information is not intended to identify you by name. A persistent pseudonymous identifier may be used to distinguish one installation or player from another and to calculate statistics such as progression and return rates.

3. Why we collect gameplay data

Gameplay telemetry may be used to:

  • improve puzzle and level balancing;
  • identify levels that are unexpectedly difficult or confusing;
  • understand where players stop playing;
  • evaluate game progression and reward systems;
  • improve the Daily Protocol feature;
  • identify bugs and technical problems;
  • evaluate usability and stability;
  • develop and improve future versions of Escape Protocol.

We do not use gameplay telemetry to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Local storage and offline use

Escape Protocol is designed to continue functioning when an internet connection is unavailable.

Certain information may be stored locally on your device, including:

  • game progress;
  • stars;
  • unlocked and selected cosmetics;
  • settings;
  • Daily Protocol progress;
  • streak information;
  • pending telemetry data.

Telemetry may first be stored locally and synchronized with our backend when an internet connection becomes available. Failure to upload telemetry does not prevent you from playing the Game.

5. Supabase

We use Supabase to provide backend infrastructure for telemetry and related Game functionality.

Gameplay data sent to our backend may therefore be processed and stored using Supabase infrastructure. Supabase and its infrastructure providers may also process technical information necessary to provide and secure their services, such as network request information.

We do not provide Supabase with your name or email address through the Game’s telemetry system.

6. Feedback

Escape Protocol includes a feedback feature.

If you choose to submit feedback, we may collect:

  • the feedback text you enter;
  • the Game version and build;
  • the level or Game context associated with the feedback, where applicable;
  • your pseudonymous Game identifier.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in feedback submissions. Feedback is used to improve, troubleshoot and develop the Game.

7. In-app purchases

Escape Protocol may offer optional digital purchases, including cosmetic content such as the Certified Baller skin.

Purchases are processed using Google Play’s billing system. Google processes payment information directly. Escape Protocol does not receive or store your full payment card details.

The Game may receive and store information necessary to verify and restore purchases, such as whether an item is owned, entitlement status, and a purchase token or transaction-related identifier where required for purchase verification.

Purchasing cosmetic content does not change puzzle difficulty or provide a gameplay advantage unless explicitly stated otherwise.

8. Advertising

The current version of Escape Protocol does not use third-party advertising SDKs or display advertising.

If advertising is added in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the relevant Google Play disclosures will be updated accordingly.

9. Legal basis for processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) or similar legislation applies, we may process gameplay and diagnostic information where necessary for our legitimate interests in:

  • operating and securing the Game;
  • improving game balance and usability;
  • diagnosing errors;
  • developing and improving the Game.

Where applicable law requires consent for a particular type of processing, we will request consent before carrying out that processing.

10. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may provide information to service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the Game, including:

  • Supabase, for backend hosting, authentication and telemetry processing;
  • Google Play, for application distribution and purchase processing.

We may also disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights or integrity of the Game or its users.

11. Data retention

Raw pseudonymous gameplay telemetry may be retained for up to 24 months, after which it may be deleted or converted into aggregated statistics that can no longer reasonably be associated with an individual player.

Feedback may be retained for as long as necessary to investigate and resolve the issue or suggestion to which it relates.

Purchase entitlement information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide, verify or restore purchased content.

Local save data remains on your device until it is deleted, application data is cleared, normal Game functionality removes it, or the Game is uninstalled, subject to Android or Google backup behaviour.

12. Data deletion and your rights

You may request deletion of telemetry or feedback associated with your pseudonymous Game identifier by contacting us using the details in the Contact section below.

Where applicable law provides these rights, you may also have the right to:

  • request access to your personal information;
  • request correction;
  • request deletion;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • request data portability;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

Because Escape Protocol does not require a conventional account with a name or email address, we may be unable to associate backend telemetry with you unless you provide the relevant pseudonymous identifier.

13. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information handled by the Game. These measures may include:

  • encrypted HTTPS/TLS communication;
  • restricted backend access;
  • authentication and authorization controls;
  • database access policies;
  • limited collection of personal information;
  • separation between gameplay save data and telemetry systems.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14. International data processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country in which you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers of personal information in accordance with applicable data-protection law.

15. Children’s privacy

Escape Protocol does not intentionally request a player’s real name, email address, location or other direct identifying information.

We do not knowingly use the Game to collect personal information from children for advertising or profiling purposes.

If you believe personal information relating to a child has been submitted through the feedback system or otherwise collected improperly, contact us using the details below so that we can investigate and, where appropriate, delete it.

16. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, our service providers or applicable legal requirements change.

The date at the top of this page indicates when the Privacy Policy was most recently updated. Material changes will be communicated in an appropriate manner where required.
